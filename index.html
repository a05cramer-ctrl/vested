On pump.fun today, a dev keeps earning creator fees even after he dumps his whole bag on you. Vested adds one condition.

01

Fees vest in a vault

Every trade's creator fee goes into the coin's vesting vault, not straight to the dev's wallet.

02

Weekly unlock

Once a week the dev can claim what has vested, but only if his wallet still holds at least his launch bag.

03

Sell and it's forfeited

If he sells below his launch bag, his unvested pay switches to buying back the coin for holders. For good.
